# K1 Score - Daily Report

**Project:** automatic scoring of K-1 kickboxing fights with computer vision
**Date:** 2026-11-18 (day 3)
**Status at end of day:** first complete pipeline (video to skeletons to strikes to points to decision) works end to end as a rule-based baseline, model v0.0.1; its accuracy is not measured yet

---

## 1. Summary

Day 3 built **model v0.0.1**: a simple, rule-based baseline for stages 2 and 3, with no training and no labels. It finds strikes from limb movement, guesses hit / blocked / miss from distances between the striking limb and the opponent's head, body, arms and legs, and turns the hits into round points and a decision with the 10-point must system.

On the 10-minute Muay Thai fight it found 224 strikes (43 hits, 96 blocked, 82 misses, 3 unknown); on the 60-second K-1 clip it found 24. The automated tests grew from 21 to 34, all passing. **Not known:** how many of these labels are correct, because there is no hand-checked set yet.

## 2. Goal of the day

Move to stage 2 with a weak but complete base, so that every later, better model has something to be measured against.

---

## 3. Work done

**Strike finder.** For every hand, foot and knee the position relative to its own shoulder or hip is compared with its normal ("rest") position over the last two seconds, in torso lengths. Body movement, camera pan and zoom cancel out. A fast peak of this deviation, moving toward an opponent who is within reach, is a strike. Knees are told from kicks by the direction of the shin; kick height comes from the foot against the opponent's shoulders and hips. Detections of the same fighter closer than 0.2 s are merged.

**Outcome rules.** At the closest approach the striking tip is compared with the opponent's head, body and (for kicks) legs. The guard is the forearms and upper arms, plus a raised shin: a thigh far from vertical means the leg is lifted for a check (the thigh-angle idea from the discussion). Distances become probabilities for hit, blocked and miss. If the skeleton of either fighter is not valid around the strike, the outcome is `unknown` and the strike is not scored.

**Strike table.** `data/events/<id>/strikes.parquet` has one row per strike (frames, times, attacker, limb, type, target, outcome, probabilities, confidence, pose quality, model version) and `frames.parquet` is a per-frame lookup.

**Scoring.** Hits earn points by type (placeholder weights), blocked strikes earn none. Rounds come from a configured length or boundaries; the 10-point must system gives 10-9, 10-8 or 10-10 rounds, and the totals give the decision or a draw. The report states how many strikes were not scored.

**Tools and tests.** New commands `k1 strikes run | samples | render` and `k1 score`. Thirteen new tests use synthetic skeletons (no strike when standing still, one punch found, hit / block / miss, missing defender, scoring rules, round splitting).

**Documentation.** `docs/model_v0.0.1.md` describes the method, the table and the weaknesses.

---

## 4. Problems found and how they were handled

**Strikes dropped when the defender is missing.** A first test expected an `unknown` strike when the defender's skeleton was missing for seven frames. The finder found nothing, because without the defender, direction and reach cannot be judged. Decision: a gap of up to five frames is interpolated (the strike is kept but its outcome stays `unknown`); a longer gap means no strike is claimed. The test now covers both cases.

**A tail of the clip became a round.** Splitting a 60.03-second clip into 20-second rounds gave a fourth, empty round. Fixed: a tail shorter than one second is part of the last round.

**A suspicious number.** About 43% of all strikes come out as blocked. The defender's arms are nearly always close to the head, so the guard rule probably fires too easily. This is written down as a known weakness and is the first thing to check against hand labels.

**Check by eye.** A contact sheet of 12 random strikes from the K-1 clip showed the marks sitting on real punches. This checks only the strike finder, not the outcome labels.

## 6. Known limits at end of day

- Hit versus blocked is not validated; blocked looks too frequent.
- 2D has no depth, the glove is not part of the skeleton, and there is no reaction or sound cue yet.
- Punch types are not separated (one class `punch`); clinch strikes and strikes toward the camera are unreliable.
- Thresholds are first guesses; scoring weights and the 10-8 rule are placeholders; rounds are not detected.
- A strike is lost when the defender's skeleton is missing for more than five frames around it.
